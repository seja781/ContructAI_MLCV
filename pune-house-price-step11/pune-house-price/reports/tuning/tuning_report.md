# Step 7: grouped-CV LightGBM comparison

The saved LightGBM settings were re-evaluated after switching to five-fold,
price-stratified grouped cross-validation on the rebuilt training set. Listings
with the same locality, BHK, property type and 25 sqft area band stay in one fold.
The fixed random test set was not used for this comparison.

| Settings | MAE (lakh) | RMSE (lakh) | MAPE (%) | R² | Within 20% (%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Step 6 LightGBM | 18.94 | 40.02 | 14.81 | 0.93 | 74.72 |
| Saved tuned LightGBM | 18.35 | 38.82 | 14.47 | 0.93 | 76.01 |

The saved tuned model is better on all reported validation measures. The
MAE reduction is 0.59 lakh (3.1%) compared with the step 6 settings.

## Residual-learning check

A three-fold grouped outer CV was used; each residual learner was trained on
out-of-fold residuals generated by a three-fold grouped inner CV. Applying the
residual correction did not help:

| Correction scale | MAE (lakh) | RMSE (lakh) | MAPE (%) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 (tuned LightGBM only) | 18.731 | 39.693 | 14.730 |
| 0.5 | 18.762 | 39.680 | 14.743 |
| 1.0 | 18.888 | 39.874 | 14.822 |

The residual model was not added to the deployed model.

The existing `tuning_results.csv` and `tuning_trials.png` are from the
earlier 30-trial random-row search (MAE 17.42 → 16.67 lakh). Those figures are
not comparable to the grouped-CV scores above and should not be read as
generalization estimates. The full 30-trial grouped search was attempted but stopped before completion
because of its long runtime; the existing saved settings were compared directly
against step 6 settings using the stricter folds.

Best settings remain in `data/config.json` → `models.lightgbm_tuned`.

## Rule-based baseline check

The comparable-rate baseline was also checked on the same grouped folds. A broader
log-area kernel and a stronger shrinkage to the locality/BHK prior improved every
grouped fold, although the random holdout score was slightly worse:

| Area bandwidth / prior strength | Grouped-CV MAE | Grouped-CV RMSE | Grouped-CV MAPE | Random-holdout MAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Previous: 0.02 / 1 | 24.88 lakh | 51.47 lakh | 19.43% | 22.37 lakh |
| Selected: 0.08 / 5 | 24.40 lakh | 50.52 lakh | 19.01% | 23.09 lakh |

Other tested alternatives (collapsing property type, changing area-rate scaling,
and weighted-mean rate aggregation) did not improve grouped-CV MAE. This is a
small cross-validation gain, not a path to zero error; it does not justify
claiming a holdout improvement.
